Toona Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 19, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Toona is designed so that its core breathing features and records stay on your device. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect, store, or transmit your personal information, breathing records, or routine data to our own servers.

Your breathing records, preferences, and custom patterns are stored locally on your device using Apple's native storage frameworks.

2. In-App Purchases

Purchases used to unlock Toona Pro are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details, billing address, or full payment information.

Apple may process transaction-related information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

3. Third-Party Services

Toona does not use third-party analytics trackers, advertising SDKs, or crash-reporting tools that collect your personal data from the app.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If the app’s data practices change in the future, this page will be updated accordingly. We encourage you to review it periodically.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer through the App Store feedback channel or the support contact information published on the Toona website.
