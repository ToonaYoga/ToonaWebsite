User Guide: Toona – Breathe Better, Live Calmer

1. Introduction to Toona

Toona is built on a singular core philosophy: Breathe Better, Live Calmer. We combine science-backed breathing patterns with immersive, high-fidelity visuals and deep progress analytics to help you reduce stress, improve sleep, and sharpen your focus. Whether you are seeking a mid-day reset or a deep evening wind-down, Toona provides the tools to build a sustainable, life-changing breathing practice.

* Device: iPhone and iPad
* Operating System: iOS 18.6+ or iPadOS 18.6+ only

2. Establishing Your Breathing Baseline

To ensure your practice is both safe and effective, Toona begins by measuring your physiological capacity through the Breathing Baseline (CO2 Test).

* The CO2 Test: This assessment measures your breath-holding capacity to determine your current tolerance levels. By establishing this baseline, the app ensures your training rhythms are challenging enough to build resilience without causing strain.
* Assessment Process: During the test, the app records your raw "Max Hold" time. For instance, if you hold your breath for 16.2 seconds, the app identifies a rounded 16s Max Hold. From this, it calculates a precise Target Hold (e.g., 5 seconds) to use during your sessions.
* Sync Your Results: Instantly sync your results to your training profile upon completion. While the app automatically adjusts your training targets based on these results, you can manually fine-tune your pace at any time in the Settings menu.

4. Visual and Auditory Immersion

Toona uses high-contrast, intuitive sensory cues to keep you centered without the need for complex menus.

Visual Guides

Select from three distinct visual modes to guide your session:

* Natural Lungs: An anatomical visualization showing the expansion and contraction of the respiratory system.
* Minimalist Circles: A clean, geometric interface that expands and shrinks in perfect rhythm with your breath.
* Full-Body View: A silhouette that features a horizontal fill line representing your breath depth. A clear countdown timer and phase label (e.g., "INHALE 03s") are overlaid on the torso, providing an at-a-glance status of your progress.

Environment & Soundscapes

Enhance your immersion by toggling ambient soundscapes that follow your breathing phases. Available options include:

* None (Silent Practice)
* White Noise
* Wind
* Rain

For a truly eyes-free experience, enable haptic feedback and voice cues to stay in rhythm through touch and sound alone.

5. Personalization and Phase Control

Toona gives you granular control over the four-part breathing cycle. Slide the "Breathing Phases" sliders in Settings to modify your experience.

Phase Definitions

1. Inhale: The time spent drawing breath in.
2. Hold (Full): The pause at the top of the breath (lungs full).
3. Exhale: The time spent releasing the breath.
4. Hold (Empty): The pause at the bottom of the breath (lungs empty).

The app automatically calculates the Per Cycle time as you adjust (e.g., a 24-second total cycle might consist of a 5s Inhale, 9s Hold, 4s Exhale, and 6s Hold).

6. Insights and Progress Tracking

Monitor your consistency and growth through our detailed analytics suite.

Weekly Insight Report

Track your dedication with a high-level summary of your weekly activity:

* Total Practice Time: Your aggregated minutes spent breathing (e.g., 20m).
* Growth Metrics: See your improvement at a glance (e.g., 79% weekly growth).
* Sessions: The number of times you opened the app to practice (e.g., 17 sessions).
* Rounds Finished: The total number of breathing cycles completed (e.g., 111 rounds).
* Active Days: Your consistency streak (e.g., 7/7 days).
* Weekly Trend: A daily bar chart visualizing your practice minutes to help you identify your most consistent days.

Phase Distribution Analysis

The integrated donut chart visualizes the time spent on Inhale vs. Hold vs. Exhale. This is a vital tool for ensuring you aren't rushing through your holds, allowing you to balance your practice for maximum physiological benefit.

History Logs

The History section provides a permanent archive of your journey. Each entry includes the date, time, number of rounds, and specific assessment scores—such as your latest CO2 Test results—allowing you to track your increasing breath capacity over time.

7. Educational Insights

Toona provides science-backed snippets to deepen your understanding of the "Why" behind the "How":

* The Vagus Nerve: Known as your body's 'Off' switch. By practicing longer exhales, you stimulate this nerve to facilitate a calmer, steadier heart rate.

* Settled Rhythm: A slower, even tempo makes breathing feel more stable, helping you transition from a state of stress to one of "Even Tempo."
