February 2026
💡 Idea

The spark

Frustrated by overcomplicated wellness apps, I wanted something simple — just breathe. Sketched the first wireframes on a Sunday afternoon.

February – March 2026
🔨 Building

First working prototype

Core breathing engine, visual circle animation, and the first preset patterns — Box, 4-7-8, Resonance.

  • Breathing phase engine (Inhale / Hold / Exhale)
  • Animated circle visualizer
  • Dark & Light mode
  • Custom pattern builder
Late March 2026
⏳ Under Review

v1.0.0 — Submitted for Review

Submitted to the App Store. Two months of work, one deep breath.

  • 5 breathing presets
  • Fully custom patterns
  • Progress tracking & weekly stats
  • Soundscapes (White noise, Wind, Rain)
  • CO₂ tolerance test
