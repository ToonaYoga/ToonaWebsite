What we stand for

Three principles guide everything we build at Toona.

🧘

Simplicity

Breathing should feel effortless. We remove friction so you can focus on what matters — the breath itself.

🔬

Science-backed

Every pattern in Toona is grounded in research. Box breathing, 4-7-8, resonance — these techniques have real, measurable effects.

🌱

Consistency

One session won't change your life. A daily habit will. We design every feature to help you show up, day after day.

📖 How it started

Born from a personal need.

Toona started as a personal project — a simple tool to practice box breathing before stressful meetings. What began as a few lines of code quickly grew into something more: a full breathing companion that adapts to your pace, your goals, and your life.

It started with a moment of frustration. Instead of letting anger take over, I took a few deep breaths — and it worked. That simple act of conscious breathing changed how I responded to stress. I wanted to build something that made it easy for anyone to do the same.

The little fish in our logo isn't just a mascot. It's a reminder that even underwater, life finds a rhythm. Toona is here to help you find yours.
